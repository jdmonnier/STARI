From an idea to an instrument

Help build the tools for discovery

Support can help strengthen STARI today and lay the groundwork for future science missions. Talk with the team about these opportunities.

Support the people behind STARI

Help support the scientists, engineers, and students developing STARI, with opportunities for hands-on research, training, and mission work.

Reduce risk and equip the mission

Fund risk reduction work, including additional testing and validation, and help purchase space-qualified hardware for STARI.

Expand onboard instrumentation

Help explore additions to STARI’s onboard instrumentation, such as webcams, that could provide more views of the spacecraft and their operations.

Enable an extended mission

Help fund operations and analysis beyond STARI’s primary mission, creating opportunities for additional experiments and demonstrations if spacecraft health and resources allow.

Prototype the next science mission

Support laboratory prototyping and testing of instruments and optical systems for a follow-on space interferometry science mission.

Explore exoplanet and black hole missions

Support preliminary design studies for a future space interferometry mission focused on exoplanets or black holes, connecting ambitious science goals with instrument and mission concepts.
