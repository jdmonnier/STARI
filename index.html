Mission Overview

Updated October 2026 · See the 2026 mission overview and formation-flying study (PDF).

Objective

STARI is a NASA-funded mission developing two 8U CubeSats as a pathfinder for future space-based optical interferometers. It aims to transfer starlight between spacecraft and couple the received beam into a single-mode optical fiber, demonstrating technologies for future exoplanet-imaging missions.

Core Technologies

The design combines optical payloads, fast-steering mirrors, beacon tracking, differential satellite navigation, and cold-gas propulsion. The 2026 guidance, navigation, and control study reports sub-millimeter optical-path knowledge and sub-centimeter control in simulations; in-orbit validation is a mission goal.

Operations & Timeline

The team completed an internal Preliminary Design Review at RPI in May 2026. Launch remains planned for 2029, with a concept of operations that alternates between standby, transfer, and science configurations in a sun-synchronous low Earth orbit.
